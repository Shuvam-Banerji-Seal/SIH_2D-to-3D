\paragraph{Negative control.} A synthetic clip of a camera rotating about its centre over one real 4K frame (\texttt{tools/make\_degenerate\_clip.py}) has no parallax by construction. It yields SNR 1.95 (median residual 0.09$^\circ$) and the verdict \emph{weak-3d}; GRIC nonetheless preferred $F$ on 100\% of its pairs, which is why GRIC only confirms the SNR decision.
